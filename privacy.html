Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Who I am

Saskia's Remote Services is operated by Saskia Armstrong, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom.

For questions about this privacy notice or how your personal information is handled, please email: saskiasremoteservices@gmail.com .

Information I collect

When you contact me through this website, by email or through a discovery-call booking service, I may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business or organisation name
  • Your telephone number, if you provide it
  • The services you are interested in
  • Information included in your enquiry
  • Your preferred appointment details
  • Records of my communications with you

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, medical information or other sensitive personal information through the website contact form.

How I use your information

I may use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Arrange and manage discovery calls
  • Prepare a quotation or service proposal
  • Provide services you have requested
  • Manage my relationship with clients
  • Maintain appropriate business and financial records
  • Protect my business and comply with legal obligations

Lawful bases

Depending on the circumstances, I process personal information because:

  • It is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract or to perform a contract
  • I have a legitimate interest in responding to enquiries, operating my business and protecting my services
  • I need to comply with a legal obligation
  • You have provided consent where consent is required

Service providers

I may use trusted service providers to operate the website and business. These may include:

  • GitHub Pages for website hosting
  • Formspree for processing website enquiries
  • Calendly for discovery-call bookings
  • My email provider for business communications
  • Accounting or invoicing software used to maintain records

These providers may process personal information on my behalf or under their own privacy terms. Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

How long I keep information

Enquiries that do not result in a client relationship will normally be deleted within 12 months, unless there is a legitimate reason to keep them for longer.

Client service records will normally be retained for up to six years after the working relationship ends where needed for tax, contractual or legal purposes.

Information held only within a client's own systems will be returned, deleted or made inaccessible at the end of the engagement in accordance with the client agreement and any applicable legal requirements.

How I protect your information

I use reasonable organisational and technical security measures, including access controls, strong unique passwords, two-factor authentication, secure devices and limited access to client information.

No method of transmitting or storing information online is completely risk-free, but I take reasonable steps to protect the information entrusted to me.

Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Ask for your information to be erased
  • Ask for processing to be restricted
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request the transfer of certain information
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions. To make a request, email: saskiasremoteservices@gmail.com .

Complaints

Please contact me first if you have concerns about how your information has been handled.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Information about raising a concern is available at ico.org.uk .

Changes to this notice

This privacy notice may be updated when my services, systems or legal responsibilities change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
